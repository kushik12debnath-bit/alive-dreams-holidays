About Us

Who We Are

A modern travel house, built on old-fashioned care. Alive Dreams Holidays is a Kolkata-based tours and travel company dedicated to designing meaningful journeys across India and the world. Though young as a brand, our leadership brings decades of hands-on travel expertise — from curating intimate family escapes and honeymoon getaways to orchestrating large corporate group movements.

We work as a small, attentive team, which means every traveller is personally looked after — not lost in a queue.
